\subsection*{F.3 \quad Normalization Ablation}
\label{app:hyperparameters_norm}

All three conditions: Split-CIFAR10 HN, \texttt{iFOPNG} only,
chunk\,=\,6\,000, $d_h$\,=\,32, $\eta = 10^{-3}$.
Conditions differ only in which rescaling operations are active:
no normalisation (\texttt{normalize\,=\,False}), gradient-only
(QR on $G$, raw Fisher), and full normalisation (QR on $G$ plus
maximum-entry Fisher scaling).

\subsection*{F.4 \quad Sub-RQ4: Fisher Accumulation Operator}
\label{app:hyperparameters_rq4}

Both operators use 20-task Permuted-MNIST, \texttt{TargetNetwork},
\texttt{normalize = False}, $\eta = 10^{-4}$, $\lambda = 10^{-2}$,
80 grads/task, full Fisher (60\,000 samples), batch\,=\,10, 5 epochs/task,
$n = 5$ seeds $\{42, 111, 811, 1234, 2137\}$.
MAX uses the element-wise maximum; EMA uses $\alpha = 0.5$.

\subsection*{F.5 \quad Compression Ablation}
\label{app:hyperparameters_compression}

Same setup as F.4 except $\lambda = 10^{-3}$, $\alpha = 0.3$ (internal
$F_{\mathrm{old}}$ EMA), and \texttt{max\_dirs}\,=\,400.
The three strategies (SVD, FIFO, STOP) are otherwise identical.
